# Daily & Weekly News Radar

## Trigger
Use for daily news, weekly news, market radar, industry developments or source-list monitoring.

## Research window
- Daily: last 24 hours.
- Weekly: last 7 days.
- If the user specifies another period, follow it.
- Always show publication date and event date separately where relevant.

## Workflow
1. Read the existing Notion source list and Market Radar structure before research.
2. Search the listed sources and relevant wider web sources.
3. Prioritise US title insurance, title search, mortgage/lending, property records, real estate, PropTech, AI document processing, fraud, regulation and operational technology.
4. Find 5–7 high-value items, not filler.
5. Open original sources and verify key claims. Cross-check major statistics or policy claims.
6. Remove duplicates, recycled stories and weakly relevant items.
7. Explain the business impact as analysis, not fact.
8. Create one practical content angle per item.
9. Find up to 3 new editorial/guest-post prospects only when they are genuinely relevant and not already in the Notion list.
10. Present results in chat for human approval. Only then update Notion.

## Required format
- Headline + publisher + date
- What happened (2 short sentences)
- Why it matters to the audience
- Possible ripple effect across the workflow
- What it could mean for I2I
- Content opportunity (blog / guest article / LinkedIn / video)
- Source link
- Confidence: High / Medium / Low, with reason

## Quality rules
- Prefer official sources, regulators, associations and reputable trade publications.
- Do not confuse a forecast, opinion or company claim with a confirmed event.
- Do not force every item into an I2I pitch.
- If fewer than five good items exist, return fewer and explain why.
- Respect site terms, access controls and copyright. Do not claim to scrape inaccessible sites.
